abstract: >
  The demand for biomedical data science in industry, academia, and government is rapidly growing, and biomedical data are being generated at a fast pace. However, successfully identifying patterns or predicting outcomes using these data is challenging. This course focuses on the fundamental statistical and machine learning concepts for biomedical data science, providing students with a precise characterization of problems and algorithms for learning from biomedical data. Topics range from biomedical data processing and data reduction to fundamentals in probability, statistical inference, and unsupervised and supervised learning. Applications include genomics, imaging, and time-series data.
